# Stage 4 Acceptance

Before Production readiness, verify with a controlled customer and then an explicitly authorized real customer if required:

- first screen answers the five 30-second questions;
- depth disclosure and evidence trace work;
- sparse customers produce more unknowns and lower confidence;
- hypotheses do not appear as facts;
- current and historical values remain separate;
- refresh is idempotent and debounced;
- stale and failed states retain the last fresh payload;
- ADMIN and scoped MENTOR access follow the existing rules;
- no Stage 3 workflow, clinical lens or sales recommendation is introduced.
