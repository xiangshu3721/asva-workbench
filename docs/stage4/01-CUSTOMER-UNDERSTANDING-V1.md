# CustomerUnderstandingV1

The service turns the existing current snapshot, materialized profile, confirmed evidence, life events, reliable self-meaning, observations and open conflicts into ten bounded modules:

1. one-line understanding
2. top issues
3. current life phase
4. current needs
5. core blocks
6. resources and strengths
7. key tensions
8. knowledge gaps
9. next conversation
10. service cautions

Each important insight carries `evidence_ids`, `confidence` and a type (`FACT_BASED`, `SELF_MEANING_BASED`, `SYNTHESIS`, or `WORKING_HYPOTHESIS`). The output is descriptive, not a personality, clinical, sales or development-stage assessment.
