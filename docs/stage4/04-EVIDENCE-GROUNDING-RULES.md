# Evidence Grounding Rules

- Confirmed FACT and current snapshot are the strongest inputs.
- Customer first-party meaning, goals and needs remain attributable to the customer.
- Observations are used as observations, not converted into facts without support.
- Hypotheses stay isolated and visually weak.
- Historical events cannot silently become current state.
- Open conflicts remain uncertain; the engine cannot choose a side.
- Important output without evidence is rejected unless it is explicitly low-confidence unknown handling.
- Diagnosis, personality typing, clinical risk and course or sales recommendations are blocked in Stage 4.
