# Customer Understanding UI

Customer Detail replaces the old standalone AI Core Summary with a compact AI Customer Understanding panel. The first screen shows the one-line understanding, up to three issues, current life phase, current needs and resources. A disclosure section contains blocks, tensions, knowledge gaps, next conversation and service cautions.

Every meaningful item can expand “查看依据” to show a source title and short evidence excerpt. Internal IDs are not shown in normal UI. Working hypotheses are dashed and marked as low-confidence. Previous fresh output remains visible while a stale or failed refresh is shown as a status, so Customer Detail is never blocked by AI generation.
